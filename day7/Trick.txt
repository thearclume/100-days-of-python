#Breaking the problem into a flowchart

Start
  ↓
Choose a random word
  ↓
Display blanks
  ↓
Ask player to guess a letter
  ↓
Is the letter in the word?
  ├── Yes → Reveal the letter
  └── No  → Reduce lives
  ↓
Has the player guessed the word?
  ├── Yes → You Win
  └── No
        ↓
Are lives remaining?
  ├── Yes → Continue guessing
  └── No  → Game Over
